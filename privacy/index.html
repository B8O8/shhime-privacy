Shhime Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · النسخة العربية

Shhime is developed and maintained by Charbel Makhlouf.

Shhime is an offline application for accessing and listening to liturgical audio content. This policy applies to the Android application org.shhime.shhime.

Information We Collect

Shhime does not collect personal information from users, and it does not transmit any information to the developer.

The application does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information.

Shhime does not collect or transmit:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Device identifiers used for tracking
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Payment information
  • Usage analytics
  • Audio recorded from the user (the app does not use the microphone)
  • Browsing activity

Internet Access

The current version of Shhime is designed to operate offline. It does not request Android's internet permission, so the application itself cannot send or receive data over the network.

Shhime does not transmit your listening activity or personal information to any developer-operated server. The developer does not operate a server for this application.

Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, Shhime stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Selected liturgical season
  • Favorites
  • Playlists
  • Bookmarks, including any note you add to them
  • Recently played recordings
  • Playback position and the last listening queue
  • Playback preferences, such as speed and repeat mode
  • Application settings, such as light or dark appearance

This information is not sent to the developer. It leaves your device only in the two situations described under “Backup and Export” below.

Backup and Export

Backup files you create. If you choose “save a backup” in the app, Shhime writes a file containing your favorites, playlists, bookmarks and listening history to the location you select with your device's file picker. If you choose “restore a backup”, Shhime reads only the file you select. Shhime does not upload these files anywhere.

If you copy, upload or share a backup file using another application or service, the privacy practices of that service apply.

Android device backup. If you have enabled Android's own backup or device-to-device transfer in your device settings, Android may include Shhime's small settings file (the items listed above) in the backup of your own Google account or in the transfer to your new device, so that they return after you reinstall the app or change phones. This is an Android system feature that you control in your device settings. The developer never receives this data. The audio recordings are not part of that backup.

Audio Content

The audio recordings included with Shhime are stored locally as part of the application's installed content and can be played without an internet connection.

The application does not monitor, report or transmit which recordings you listen to.

Analytics and Advertising

Shhime does not use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Behavioral advertising
  • Analytics platforms
  • Crash-reporting services
  • User tracking technologies
  • Third-party tracking SDKs

Shhime does not sell personal data and does not share personal data for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

Shhime does not require a user account and does not collect personal information from anyone, whether a child or an adult. Accordingly, no personal information is knowingly collected from children through the application.

Permissions

Shhime uses only the device capabilities needed for its functions:

  • Audio playback, including continuing playback in the background as a media-playback service while you listen
  • Keeping the device awake while audio is playing
  • The playback notification and media controls (notification, lock screen, headset buttons)
  • File access that you start yourself, through the system file picker, to save or restore a backup

The application does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or general device storage.

Data Security

Because Shhime does not transmit personal information to a developer-operated server, the application's data is protected primarily by the security mechanisms of your device and of the Android operating system.

You should protect access to your device and to any backup files you create.

Third-Party Services

The current application does not rely on third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services. It includes open-source software components for audio playback and local storage; these components run on your device and do not send data to anyone.

If you obtain the application through an app store such as Google Play, that store's handling of your download and account information is governed by its own privacy policy.

If any of this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling Shhime, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes the information it stored on your device. Backup files you saved yourself remain where you saved them until you delete them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Shhime's functionality or data practices change. Any updated version will show a revised effective date.
